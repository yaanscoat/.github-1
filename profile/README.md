<h1 align="center">wasmdotrip</h1>

<p align="center"> Welcome to wasm.rip!! We're a group of dedicated teens making webports of popular games, you may have played some of them before! </p>

## Projects

Here's a list of things we've been working on

- [Among Us](https://wasm.rip/files/amongus/index.html) - Group Project
- [Azahar Web](https://wasm.rip/files/Azahar/index.html) - port by SomeRandomFella
- [Bendy and the Ink Machine](https://wasm.rip/files/BATIM/index.html) - port by crackers and slqnt
- [Clustertruck](https://wasm.rip/files/ClusterTruck/clustertruck.html) - port by NotRexxed
- [Cuphead](https://wasm.rip/files/cuphead/index.html) - port by crackers
- [Granny 3](https://wasm.rip/files/granny/granny3.html) - port by crax
- [GTA III](https://wasm.rip/files/gta3/index.html) - port by shxyder
- [How To fish](https://wasm.rip/files/HowToFish/HowToFish.html) - port by NotRexxed

To view a full list of our projects, visit our website [wasm.rip](https://wasm.rip)
